Your Questions, Answered

Where is Hotel Narayani International located?

The hotel is on Raipur Road near Gurunala Gate, Kawardha, Kabirdham district, Chhattisgarh 491995, India.

How can I book a room?

Call the hotel on 076490 00400 or 076490 00500 to ask about room availability, rates and reservations.

What room types are available?

Booking listings show Deluxe AC Room, Premium Room and Narayani Suite options. Please check directly with the hotel for current room types, features and availability.

What facilities and dining options are available?

Online hotel listings mention air-conditioned rooms, Wi-Fi, a restaurant, swimming pool and gym. Facilities, meal options and availability can change; confirm them with the hotel before booking.

Which places can I visit near the hotel?

Nearby places of interest include Kawardha Palace, Bhoramdeo Temple, Madwa Mahal and Saroda Dam. Check local travel conditions and opening details before visiting.

How can I change or cancel my booking?

Contact the hotel or the service you booked through to request changes or cancellation. Applicable deadlines and charges depend on your reservation; confirm the terms shown for your booking.
